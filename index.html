Section 01
Executive Summary

Meridian Analytics delivered a strong third quarter, reporting total revenue of $847 million, representing 12% year-over-year growth driven by continued momentum across our Enterprise and Mid-Market segments. Operating income reached $203 million, reflecting disciplined cost management and improving unit economics as our platform matures. Net margin expanded to 24%, up from 21% in the prior-year period, underscoring the operating leverage inherent in our subscription-based business model.

Customer acquisition remained robust, with our installed base growing 18% year-over-year to over 14,200 active accounts globally. Enterprise adoption continued to accelerate, with average contract values increasing 9% as customers expanded their usage across additional business units and geographies. Our net revenue retention rate held at 118%, reflecting the stickiness of our platform and the success of our customer success programs.

Looking ahead, management is raising full-year 2024 revenue guidance to a range of $1.12–$1.14 billion, reflecting confidence in our pipeline and the continued demand for AI-powered analytics solutions. We believe our differentiated platform, deepening partner ecosystem, and disciplined capital allocation position Meridian Analytics for sustained, profitable growth through the remainder of 2024 and into fiscal year 2025.
